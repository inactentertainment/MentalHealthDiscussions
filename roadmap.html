Navigation boundaries

Useful without pretending to be your clinician.

No diagnosis

MHD can help explain symptom patterns and questions to ask, but it does not tell you what diagnosis you have.

No prescribing

Medication pages explain common uses, monitoring, side effects, and provider questions—not personalized dosing or medication changes.

Safety first

Immediate danger, overdose, severe medical instability, or inability to stay safe should move to live crisis or emergency support rather than a website workflow.
