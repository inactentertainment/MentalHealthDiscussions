How to use it

Speak the term.

Select Speak and say the word or phrase. If browser speech recognition is available, MHD searches the transcript immediately.

Hear the pronunciation.

Every entry has a readable pronunciation cue plus a Hear word button using your browser's text-to-speech voice.

Have MHD read it to you.

Select Read definition to hear the plain-English definition aloud. Stop speech at any time.
