Provider Questions

Ask before you begin.

Training, diagnosis, protocol, intensity, goals, alternatives, cost, and follow-up all matter.

About the treatment

  • What diagnosis or symptoms are we targeting?
  • Why this treatment rather than another evidence-based option?
  • What happens in a typical session or procedure?
  • How will we measure whether it is helping?

About the provider

  • What training do you have in this specific treatment?
  • How often do you treat people with my condition?
  • Who handles medication, medical complications, or emergencies?

Risks and practical demands

  • What distress, side effects, sedation, memory effects, or other risks should I know about?
  • How many visits are typical and what happens between sessions?
  • What insurance, transportation, work leave, or caregiver support may be needed?
