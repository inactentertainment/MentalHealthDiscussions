Better mental-health information should be easier to find.

Mental Health Discussions is being built to make complex mental-health information more understandable, practical and accessible—for individuals, families, caregivers and anyone trying to navigate what comes next.

Keep resources free

Your support helps us continue offering educational guides, navigation tools and practical resources without putting essential information behind a paywall.

Build better tools

Support helps fund development, hosting, accessibility improvements, research review, site maintenance and new tools that make difficult information easier to use.

Reach more families

It helps us expand clear resources for people supporting loved ones, preparing for appointments, comparing treatment information and navigating crisis or system questions.

What you are helping us build

MHD is more than a collection of articles. The goal is a practical public resource that helps people move from confusion to understanding—from “What does this mean?” to “What can I ask?” and “Where can I go next?”

Support allows us to keep improving the research and tools, strengthen accessibility, develop family and caregiver resources, maintain the technology behind the site, and continue translating complicated information into language people can actually use.

Every contribution helps move the project closer to a larger long-term vision: a trusted, transparent and widely accessible mental-health education resource built around usefulness rather than paywalls.

Your support helps with

  • Research review and plain-language educational content
  • Medication, treatment and appointment-preparation tools
  • Family and caregiver navigation resources
  • Crisis, hospital and legal-navigation education
  • Accessibility, mobile usability and site maintenance
  • New public-facing tools and resource development

Help us keep building.

If MHD has helped you understand something, prepare for a conversation, support someone you care about or feel less lost, you can help make that same access available to someone else.
