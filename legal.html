Legal & Policies

Clear rules. Clear boundaries.

MHD is an educational project. These policies explain what the site does, what it does not do, how information is handled, and how future funding or affiliate relationships will be disclosed.

Medical Disclaimer

Mental Health Discussions provides general educational information and organizational tools. MHD does not diagnose, prescribe, establish a clinician-patient relationship, or replace care from a qualified medical or mental-health professional.

Medication, treatment, symptom, crisis, and legal-navigation information can change and may not apply to an individual situation. Do not start, stop, combine, or change medication or treatment based only on this website.

For immediate danger or a medical emergency, call 911. In the U.S., call or text 988 for mental-health crisis support.

Privacy

MHD is being designed to minimize collection of sensitive information. Current browser-based tools may process information locally in the visitor's browser. Where a tool says information is session-only, closing or refreshing the page may erase it.

The current homepage contact form prepares an inquiry locally but does not yet transmit it to MHD because the public contact-delivery endpoint has not been finalized. Do not enter emergency information into that form.

Future services that create accounts, save records, process payments, or transmit messages will require updated privacy disclosures before launch.

Terms of Use

Use MHD for education, preparation, and navigation—not as a substitute for professional diagnosis, treatment, emergency response, or individualized legal advice. Visitors are responsible for verifying time-sensitive information such as laws, hospital procedures, approvals, coverage, and service availability.

MHD may link to third-party websites. Those services have their own terms, privacy practices, and content responsibility.

Affiliate Disclosure

MHD may eventually use carefully selected affiliate relationships to help support free public educational resources. If an affiliate relationship is active, it should be clearly disclosed near the recommendation or link.

Affiliate compensation must not determine clinical conclusions, crisis recommendations, evidence ratings, or research summaries. No affiliate relationship should be presented as an endorsement of a treatment for an individual.

Copyright / DMCA

MHD respects copyright and intellectual-property rights. Original MHD text, organization, interface elements, and tools may be protected by applicable copyright law. Third-party names, trademarks, videos, and linked resources remain the property of their respective owners.

If you believe material on MHD infringes a copyright you control, use the Contact section and select Copyright / legal notice once direct message delivery is connected. A formal DMCA notice process and designated contact can be published if and when required for MHD's hosting and operations.

Accessibility

MHD aims for readable contrast, keyboard-friendly controls, scalable text, reduced-motion support, comfort color choices, and alternatives to unnecessarily stimulating presentation. Accessibility testing is an ongoing part of development.

Visitors can use the floating Comfort control to change light level, color palette, motion, and optional sound.

Funding & Transparency

MHD's educational content should remain clearly separated from advertising, affiliate compensation, sponsorship, and other funding relationships. Future financial support should be disclosed in a way that does not obscure clinical or editorial independence.

Future 501(c)(3) Roadmap

Current status: MHD is not currently a 501(c)(3) nonprofit organization. Contributions or support should not be represented as tax-deductible charitable donations.

A future nonprofit path may be explored. Any future-facing language on the site should remain clearly labeled until legal status actually changes.

Contact

Use the MHD homepage contact form for general inquiries, corrections, source questions, accessibility feedback, partnership discussions, media requests, and legal/copyright notices.
