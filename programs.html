Programs & Partnerships

Useful online. Partner-ready in the real world.

MHD is being built as a navigation platform—not another general awareness library. The goal is to help people understand what is happening, prepare better questions, organize information, move through disconnected systems, and connect to the right community or professional resource.

Programs MHD can test with partners.

These concepts are ready to be developed with a host organization, but MHD should not describe them as active recurring services until a pilot is actually scheduled and staffed.

Pilot-ready

Family Navigation Workshop

A practical educational session built around the questions families ask after symptoms appear, treatment is recommended, or a crisis changes everything.

  • How to prepare observations
  • How to ask better provider questions
  • How to organize medication and discharge information
  • Where family rights and limits commonly matter

Future initiatives

Build original value, not just more content.

These require governance, data safeguards, staffing, partner agreements, and/or enough usage to produce responsible findings.

Future initiative

MHD Navigator Ambassadors

Trained non-clinical volunteers who help people operate MHD tools, prepare questions, and locate appropriate services without diagnosing or directing treatment.

Future initiative

MHD Systems Lab

Study where users repeatedly get stuck across access, insurance, discharge, family communication, state rules, medication information, and provider navigation.

Future initiative

Navigation Scorecards

Evaluate how easy or difficult it is to navigate public mental-health information and systems using transparent criteria rather than ranking clinical outcomes.

Future initiative

Annual Navigation Report

Publish anonymized trends about what people are trying to navigate, recurring information gaps, and system friction—only after appropriate privacy and methodology standards are in place.

Future initiative

Advisory Network

Build clinical, legal/policy, lived-experience, caregiver, accessibility, and research advisors who can strengthen review and reduce blind spots.

Future initiative

Partner API / Embed Tools

Allow approved organizations to embed selected MHD navigation tools or route users into partner-specific roadmaps without duplicating the whole site.

Who MHD can complement

Partner instead of duplicate.

MHD should add a navigation layer around strong existing organizations rather than trying to replace their support groups, clinical care, screening programs, local chapters, or institutional consulting.

Peer & family organizations

MHD can provide preparation, documentation, state-law, medication, treatment, and hospital-navigation tools while the partner provides human support and community.

Hospitals & care teams

MHD can help families organize questions before appointments and turn discharge instructions into a follow-up checklist without replacing clinical recommendations.

Libraries, faith & community groups

Partners can host navigation workshops or QR stations without needing to become mental-health providers.

Schools & workplaces

MHD can offer individual and family navigation resources while schools and employers retain responsibility for their own policies, supports, and professional services.

The MHD role

What we do

  1. Translate complicated information into plain language.
  2. Help people organize what they know.
  3. Route them to the right question, tool, official source, or professional resource.
  4. Help families and individuals prepare for the next conversation.
  5. Identify recurring navigation problems that may deserve system attention.
Partnership principle

MHD should be the layer between “I know something is wrong” and “I know what to do next.”

That makes MHD useful beside established organizations rather than a smaller copy of them.
